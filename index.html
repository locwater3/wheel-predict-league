How to play in 30 seconds

  • Connect your wallet (Sepolia). Signing is free — predictions never send a transaction.
  • Each round lasts 10 minutes. Check the wheel status and history, then sign & lock your call for the next round to settle.
  • A locked prediction can't be edited. When the round settles on-chain, your score is computed from the real work / blocks in the settlement.
  • Climb the leaderboard, follow the sharpest predictors, copy their calls — and beat SqueekBot.
